M O M B A O

In a time when reference points are being overturned, like magnetic poles swapping in a dance, we Mombao see a way, an opportunity for change and rebirth. The world is changing, it has already changed, all we have left is to plant a fruit tree before the battle, to keep building until the moment before a cataclysm, to believe in a solar future even when it all seems to be falling apart. What do you see in your future? Where will we be in 10,000 years?
We MOMBAO want to learn photosynthesis, understand how plants create energy from the sun and give us life, we want to integrate their wisdom with artificial intelligences. If your job is replaceable by a computer, it means you are wasting your cognitive potential!
You are not a machine, you are an orgy of necessary randomness! Your power is the randomness, the interdependence of your cells, your cells know what is good for you, what is good for the microbiota of your gut.
We no longer have to create divisions, we want to understand how to be free within interdependence! We MOMBAO want to learn how to communicate with animals, we are animals! We want to die after understanding what a dolphin feels, what the swallow has seen when it returns to the attic after a long journey and chooses us to guard its young! We are plants, in our DNA we have the memory of all that we have been, the speed of the jaguar, the strength of the bear, the endurance of the oak, we were fish, we were fungi! We are all waves that think and organize!
So reincarnate, dance with us, close your eyes, begin to shake, feel every muscle massaging your bones.

Dance like a salmon,
like an octopus,
like a microchip,
a mycelium,
a bot,
an electron.
